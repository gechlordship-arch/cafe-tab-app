# ☕ CafeTab

A simple credit-tab system for cafes and restaurants in Ethiopia.

Customers eat first, sign with their password, and settle their balance at month end — no cash needed at every meal.

## 🎯 What It Does

- **Manager** sets up the menu, adds staff, adds customers, and tracks balances
- **Staff** records what each customer eats
- **Customer** can log in to see their own meals and total owed

## 👥 Three Roles

| Role | Login | What they can do |
|------|-------|------------------|
| **Manager** | Phone + Password | Add menu, staff, customers · mark paid · reset passwords · record meals |
| **Staff** | Phone + Password | Record meals · view customer balances |
| **Customer** | Phone + Password | View own meals and total owed |
